# Sound credits

Every sound in the book is cut from a real recording released under **CC0 1.0** (public domain dedication) on
Freesound. Attribution is not required; the sources are listed so anyone can find the originals, and each Freesound
page names the recording's creator. There is no music and nothing synthesised.

The clips were cut, high-passed, loudness-matched and packed into `sfx.mp3` by `tools/audio/cut.mjs` (cut list:
`tools/audio/clips.json`) from the originals in `audio-src/` (Freesound's HQ MP3 previews, not in git; fetched by
`tools/audio/fetch.sh`, checksums in `audio-src/SHA256SUMS`).
